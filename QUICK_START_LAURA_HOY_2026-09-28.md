## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES (AVOID THESE)

### ❌ Mistake 1: Waiting for Perfect
**Wrong:** "I'll film the Reel when I have professional lighting"
**Right:** "I'll film today with my phone. Good enough beats perfect."

**Lesson:** 80% of something published > 100% of nothing

---

### ❌ Mistake 2: Inconsistency
**Wrong:** "I'll publish 3 times this week, then 0 next week"
**Right:** "I'll publish daily, same time, rain or shine"

**Lesson:** Consistency > quality in algorithm

---

### ❌ Mistake 3: Not Responding
**Wrong:** "I'm too busy creating content to respond to comments"
**Right:** "Reply to comments first, then create content"

**Lesson:** Community > content (AI will help you scale this)

---

### ❌ Mistake 4: Not Tracking
**Wrong:** "I'll just check metrics once a week"
**Right:** "I'll update dashboard daily, adjust weekly"

**Lesson:** Data drives decisions (you don't know what's working if you don't track)

---

### ❌ Mistake 5: Giving Up Too Soon
**Wrong:** "It's been 3 days, no followers yet. This doesn't work."
**Right:** "It's been 3 days. Week 1 targets are Week 1, not Day 1."

**Lesson:** 30-day sprint, not 3-day sprint
