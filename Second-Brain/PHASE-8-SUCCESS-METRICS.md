## TASK SCORING FORMULA

**Priority Score = (Impact + Revenue + Urgency) / (Complexity * Effort)**

### Scoring Scale

**Impact:** 1-10 (business value)  
**Revenue:** 1-10 (revenue potential)  
**Urgency:** 1-10 (time sensitivity)  
**Complexity:** 1-5 (technical difficulty)  
**Effort:** hours required  

**Example:**
- Impact: 8
- Revenue: 9
- Urgency: 7
- Complexity: 2
- Effort: 4 hours
- Score: (8 + 9 + 7) / (2 * 4) = 24 / 8 = **3.0**
